Nye Warburton is Dean of the School of Creative Technology (STEC) at SCAD in Savannah. Educator / Creative Technologist / Animator / Game Developer.

He teaches artists to build for what’s coming. His mission is to prepare students for competitive careers in gaming and interactive media — with an eye toward AI workflows, networked production, and immersive technologies.

He began as a cartoonist, became a professional motion graphics artist, then a feature animator, then an entrepreneurial game developer. He has more than 20 years of experience across animated films, television, and games. Credits listed on his public CV include Monster House, Avengers, Oblivion, and Men in Black III.

As a contractor and entrepreneur, he has built apps, virtual reality, and gamified software. He has worked across startups and independent productions, and in mainstream movies, television, and games.

Now he focuses on educating and mentoring the next generation of digital artists, designers, and game developers.
